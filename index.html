How to use

Set up in four steps

No technical know-how needed. Ten minutes, once.

Put your films in two folders

On your USB drive (or the folder on your computer), make a folder called Movies and one called Series. Give every film its own folder:

Movies/ My Film (2021)/ film.mp4 the video, any name poster.jpg optional: the picture on its card Series/ My Show/ poster.jpg Season 1/ 01 First Episode.mp4 02 Second Episode.mp4

That's all it needs. Add a background.jpg for a wide picture, and an .srt file for subtitles. Folder names become the titles.

Install NEKPLIK on your TV

  1. On the TV, open the Google Play Store and install the free app Downloader.
  2. Open Downloader and type this address: github.com/knznepo/telebisyon/releases/latest/download/nekplik.apk
  3. When the TV asks, allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then press Install. If Google Play Protect warns you, choose Install anyway.
  4. Open NEKPLIK from your TV's apps.
No Downloader? Copy nekplik.apk from the Download section onto a USB stick, plug it into the TV, and open it with the TV's file manager.

Connect your films

Choose whichever is easiest. You can use more than one: NEKPLIK shows them all together.

💾 USB drive in the TV

  1. Plug the drive into the TV.
  2. Open NEKPLIK and press OK to let it read the drive.

📱 From your phone

  1. Install NEKPLIK Share on the phone.
  2. Tap Allow access to drives, then Choose a USB drive.
  3. Tap Start sharing.

💻 From your computer

  1. Open NEKPLIK Share on the Mac or PC.
  2. Choose the folder or drive that holds Movies and Series.
  3. Leave it running.
The TV, phone and computer just need to be on the same Wi-Fi. NEKPLIK finds them by itself.

Grab the popcorn

Open NEKPLIK on the TV: your films are there, in rows. Pick one and press Play.

On a phone or laptop, open the link NEKPLIK Share shows (or scan its QR code) to watch in the browser.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. NEKPLIK is free and open source (MIT licence). There are no accounts, subscriptions or ads.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything happens on your home Wi-Fi: films go straight from the drive, computer or phone to the TV.

Which video files play?

On the TV: most MP4 and MKV files. In a browser it depends on the browser:

BrowserPlays
Chrome, EdgeMP4, and most MKV
Safari (iPhone, iPad, Mac)MP4

Films a browser can't play are still listed, marked TV only.

Do I need posters and descriptions?

No. Folder names become titles. Add a poster.jpg and a small info.json with a title and description whenever you like, and NEKPLIK shows the year, score, cast and genres too.

Let AI write it for you. Paste this into Google Gemini or ChatGPT, with your film's name in place of MOVIE NAME (YEAR):

Make an info.json file for the movie or series "MOVIE NAME (YEAR)" for my NEKPLIK media library. Use these fields: title, description (one or two sentences, no spoilers), year, released (YYYY-MM-DD), rating (like PG-13), score (out of 10), runtime (in minutes), genres (a list), cast (up to 5 main actors), director, language, tagline. Leave out any field that doesn't apply. Reply with only the JSON, nothing else.

Save the answer as info.json in the film's folder, next to the video. AI can get details wrong, so give it a quick look.

Download an example info.json Open it with Notepad or TextEdit, change the details, and save.

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV: Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Xiaomi Mi Box, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield and other Android TV boxes from about 2017 on. Fire TV with Fire OS should work but hasn't been tested.

The TV doesn't see my USB drive.

Open NEKPLIK and press OK to let it read drives. If the TV can't open the drive in its own file manager either, the drive's format is the problem. Share it from a phone or computer instead: that always works.

Can several people watch at once?

Yes, each their own film, on the TV, phones and laptops at the same time.

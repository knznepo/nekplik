# NEKPLIK

NEKPLIK turns the films and shows on your USB drive, computer or phone into your own streaming app, on your Android TV and in any browser at home. No accounts, no subscriptions, nothing uploaded.

**Website:** https://knznepo.github.io/nekplik/

This repository is the website: `index.html`, the screenshots in `images/` (a demo library with made-up titles), and an example `info.json` in `examples/`. The apps themselves are in [Telebisyon](https://github.com/knznepo/telebisyon/tree/main/nekplik), and the downloads come from its [latest release](https://github.com/knznepo/telebisyon/releases/latest).

## Disclaimer

NEKPLIK is a personal media library and playback application. It does not provide, host, distribute, or include copyrighted movies or television shows. Users are responsible for ensuring that the media they access through the application is legally obtained and used.
